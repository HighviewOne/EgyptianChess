Rules of Egypt
Pharaoh (King) — One square any direction.
Vizier (Queen) — Any distance, any direction.
Chariot (Rook) — Any distance, ranks & files.
Priest (Bishop, Anubis) — Any distance, diagonals.
𓃬 Sphinx (Extended Knight) — Standard knight L-jump and slides 1–2 squares diagonally without jumping over pieces.
Soldier (Pawn) — Forward 1; 2 from start; captures diagonally. En passant applies. Promotes at far rank.
☥ Ankh Resurrection — Once per game per side: spend your turn to place your most recently lost piece on any empty square in your back two ranks. Cannot leave you in check.
Pyramid Zone (d4–e5) — Sacred ground. Positional atmosphere — no mechanical effect.
No castling. Check, checkmate, and stalemate follow classical rules.
